Behind the Challenge

How the Scrabble
challenge was made.

This challenge was designed specifically for the XB-IT Halloween event. The board is more than a collection of random letters.

01

The letters have a story.

Each letter was created from the first initial of the last name of members of our XB-IT team. The result is a custom letter rack created specifically for our event.

02

One word wins.

The challenge is intentionally designed so that finding a valid smaller word is not enough. Participants need to figure out the one word that uses the available letters to solve the challenge.

03

The board is interactive.

Tap the physical-looking Scrabble tiles on the screen to build your answer. Selected tiles lift from the board, making the challenge feel like a real tabletop game.

04

The first player wins.

Every submission is recorded. The first participant to submit the winning answer locks the challenge and becomes the Halloween Word Challenge winner.
