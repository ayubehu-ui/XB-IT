Behind the Challenge

How the Scrabble
challenge was made.

The challenge combines an interactive Scrabble board, a live submission system, and a Google Sheet backend to determine the first winner.

01

The letters were selected.

The challenge uses a fixed rack of 14 Scrabble-style tiles. The letters are intentionally scrambled on the board.

02

One word wins.

The challenge is intentionally designed so that finding a valid smaller word is not enough. Participants need to find the one winning answer.

03

The board is interactive.

Tap the physical-looking Scrabble tiles on the screen to build your answer. Selected tiles lift from the board.

04

The first player wins.

Every submission is recorded. The first participant to submit HOMEWORK locks only the Scrabble challenge and becomes the Halloween Word Challenge winner.
