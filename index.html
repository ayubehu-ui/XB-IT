Behind the challenge

Designed like a product.
Built like a game.

The experience is intentionally simple on the surface. Underneath, it combines an interactive board, live submissions, a protected first-winner check, and a Google Sheet backend.

01

Choose

Every tile is interactive. Selected letters move into the answer builder in the exact order you choose.

02

Submit

Your name and answer are sent to the challenge backend and recorded for the live activity feed.

03

Win

The first correct submission locks only the challenge and reveals the winner without taking down the event page.

⌁

Apple-inspired interaction

Large typography, restrained glass surfaces, soft depth, subtle motion, high-contrast controls, and a mobile-first layout keep the experience feeling premium instead of like a generic event form.

The hidden story

The rack was created from the first initials of XB-IT colleagues' last names. The final reveal keeps last names private while making the team connection part of the game.
